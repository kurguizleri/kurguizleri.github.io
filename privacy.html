Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who we are. Kurgu İzleri ("the Tool") is a publishing tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @kurguizleri. It is used only to share the account owner's own original content on that account. It is not offered to other users.

What data we access. When the account owner authorizes the Tool through TikTok Login Kit, we receive the account's Open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos (video.upload, and video.publish only if direct posting is enabled). We also receive the status of each upload.

What we do not access. We do not read private messages, follower lists, other users' data or viewing history. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or sale.

What we store, and for how long. Access and refresh tokens are stored in the operating system's credential store on the account owner's own computer and are deleted when access is revoked or expires. The Open ID and display name are kept only while the authorization is active. Upload IDs and statuses are kept in a local log for up to 12 months to prevent duplicate uploads. Video files and captions are our own content.

Sharing. We do not sell or share TikTok user data. Data is sent only to TikTok through its official APIs.

Your choices. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings. You may ask us to delete stored data by writing to korkmazadem245@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.

Children. The Tool is not directed to children and collects no data about them.

Changes. We update this page when our practices change and show the new date above.

Contact: korkmazadem245@gmail.com
